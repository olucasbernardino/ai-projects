# Project 01: Small businesses in Spain vs the EU

**Status:** planning done, data collection next
**Type:** made with AI · data & reporting
**Started:** 2026-11-10

## Question

How do small businesses in Spain compare with the EU average on jobs, economic
weight and business dynamics?

## Indicators (fixed, reused if the report expands)

| # | Indicator | Planned source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Share of jobs in micro and small firms (0–49 persons employed) | Eurostat structural business statistics |
| 2 | Share of value added by micro and small firms | Eurostat structural business statistics |
| 3 | Enterprise birth rate | Eurostat business demography |
| 4 | Enterprise death rate | Eurostat business demography |
| 5 | 5-year survival rate | Eurostat business demography |

Exact dataset codes, years and filters are confirmed in `sources.md` when the
data is downloaded.
